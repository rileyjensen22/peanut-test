Regular is just salt and water — pure, briny peanuts the way your grandparents made them. Cajun adds red pepper flakes, garlic, and a couple of other things. It builds slow; the heat doesn't hit until the second or third bite.
Fresh-dug green peanuts come in late summer through early fall. Outside that window we use raw dried peanuts soaked overnight — the flavor is a little different but they still take on the brine well. We'll tell you which we're running when you call.
