Slow, salty, and done the old way

  1. 01

    Green peanuts, not dried

    Fresh-dug green peanuts from late summer into fall, so the shells drink in the brine. Off-season, we use raw dried peanuts soaked overnight.

  2. 02  Hours in the kettle

    Four hours or more at a low rolling boil, tasted as we go until the kernel is tender all the way through.

  3. 03  Soaked before serving

    They rest in the warm brine so every peanut is seasoned inside the shell, not just on it.
